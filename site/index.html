About LCANA

Our purpose

The Lowcountry Area of Narcotics Anonymous serves members and groups across the South Carolina Lowcountry. Our primary purpose is to carry the message to the addict who still suffers.

NA is a fellowship of people who support one another in recovery. There are no dues or fees for membership.

Who we serve

LCANA supports meetings and members throughout the Lowcountry, including:
